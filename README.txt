Tiny World V6 — LIVING CIVILIZATION

V6 is focused on PEOPLE rather than graphics. V6.5 will be the next massive visual overhaul.

LIFE SIMULATION
- Young Child, Child, Teen, Adult, Older Adult and Elder life stages.
- Citizens age naturally.
- Individual randomized lifespans.
- Natural old-age deaths.
- Existing starvation, dehydration, hazards and injuries use the same death system.
- Deaths create grief and memories in partners, family and close friends.
- Widowed relationship state.

CHILDHOOD / EDUCATION
- Children learn from nearby parents and skilled adults.
- Mentors are tracked.
- Education grows through childhood.
- Teenagers develop work interests.
- Children inherit blended personality traits from both parents with natural variation.
- Lifespan potential is partially inherited.

CAREERS / SKILLS
- Six persistent work skills: Gathering, Woodcutting, Building, Farming, Mining and Hauling.
- Novice / Practiced / Skilled / Expert / Master progression.
- Skilled workers perform better.
- Job assignment now considers personality, experience, preference and village needs.
- Career changes become memories instead of arbitrary invisible switches.
- Skill milestones can enter World History.

SOCIAL LIFE
- Citizens track happiness, stress and grief.
- Friendships emerge from positive relationship scores.
- Rivals can emerge from repeated conflict.
- High stress / low kindness can cause arguments.
- Best friend and rival are shown in citizen information.
- Long-term goals change with age, family, grief, wealth, personality and skills.

HOUSEHOLDS / PERSONAL LIFE
- Families try to share huts.
- Citizens track a permanent home when housing exists.
- Wealth, reputation, tools and keepsakes are tracked.
- Work slowly increases personal wealth and reputation.

FAMILIES
- V5.3 marriage and surname inheritance remain.
- New World > Families registry.
- Family groups include living and deceased relatives.
- Citizen inspector shows parents, children, home, mentor, birth name and family information.

WORLD SIZES
- V5.4 100×100 / 150×150 / 200×200 / 300×300 / 500×500 remain.

NEXT
- V6.5 will be a massive graphics overhaul based on the visual reference supplied by the user.
